## Task

Introduce first-class **Anti-Ideal State Criteria** (statements that must remain false) and emit **Refusal Certificates** when the system correctly refuses action (force KILL, park, block evaluate). Certificates are durable negative knowledge queryable by later runs.

## Outer Binary Ideal State Criteria

1. **[outcome]** ≥3 Anti-ISC are written as binary, checkable negatives (examples: soft-close pursue forbidden; third soft rewrite forbidden; approve without schema validate forbidden).
2. **[outcome]** On anti-loop force-KILL and at least one parked-pack decision, a Refusal Certificate is produced: `(workflow, anti_isc_ids, evidence_ref, timestamp)`.
3. **[outcome]** Certificates are stored in CI-EM-compatible form with `mode: refusal` or equivalent.
4. **[guardrail]** Over-refusal is measured: strong pursueable the reference engine samples must **not** gain false force-KILL solely from Anti-ISC machinery.
5. **[outcome]** Certificate can be retrieved and cited in a subsequent session note (manual retrieval OK).
6. **[economic]** Certificate write ≤ one structured record per refusal event.
7. **[guardrail]** No topology mutation; refusals do not auto-delete positive skills.

### Anti-ISC text (binary negatives)

1. **n1** — A pursue verdict is not issued when POP is a soft close (brand, engagement, or see-what-happens).
2. **n2** — A third soft rewrite of the same approach is not executed; rewritePass stays below MAX_REWRITE_PASSES or the verdict is kill.
3. **n3** — A curriculum pack is not approved when schema validate has not passed.

## Key Insight

Refusal Certificates are useful **because they are not a second scorer**. They record that a refusal *already happened* for a named Anti-ISC. Wiring n1/n2 into `decideVerdict` would be over-refusal risk and a topology change; the existing anti-loop and POP scorers already implement n1/n2 in product. The certificate is the **memory**, not the **actuator**. n3 (approve without validate) is the forge-side analog of anti-loop: parking is a human τ3 decision, the cert makes it queryable at τ2.

**KEEP scope:** keep Anti-ISC list + `emitRefusalCertificate`. Do not auto-apply certificates as vetoes beyond the existing engine rules.
