## Key Insight

CI-EM weight from **already-fixed** τ1 bugs is not a valid τ3 stake. The skeleton/companion/trees failures were converted into `quality_gates` inside enrich — that *is* the confidence gate, already in the current topology. Adding another named stage would duplicate it. Separately: **schema validate does not park laser**. A topology that treated `validate OK` as approvable would be an anti-ISC regression on n3. Parking remains a human decision + Refusal Certificate, not a graph edge, until an equipment-profile schema exists (out of scope).

**SHADOW scope:** keep the proposal/shadow ritual. Do not activate. Do not edit forge pipeline.
