## Task

Run a controlled 3×5 comparison of **bare prompting**, **gxp (Phase 1)**, and **gxp-plus (Phase 1 + Phase 2 KEEP)** on the same seeds, with the same two scorecards, and record KEEP / SHADOW / ABANDON using the pre-registered ship rule. This is the live-work test that Phase 2 golden-set measurements cannot replace.

## Outer Binary Ideal State Criteria

1. **[outcome]** Protocol, seeds, condition cards, and rubric are frozen before cell 1.
2. **[outcome]** All 15 cells produce a transcript + handoff (task success may be false).
3. **[outcome]** Card A and Card B are filled per cell by a **post-hoc scorer pass** (not the worker).
4. **[outcome]** S1 plus cell records an explicit collapse-to-v3 decision (or is scored as collapse-fail).
5. **[guardrail]** No τ3 product merge: `the reference engine/engine.ts`, forge enrich/validate/approve, and parked laser are not rewritten to “activate plus.”
6. **[economic]** ≤1 worker session per cell; scorer is one additional pass over the 15 artifacts.
7. **[outcome]** Ship decision recorded against the campaign rule (opt-in / default / shadow / abandon).

## Benefit / Cost Gate (pre-registered)

| Axis | Threshold |
|------|-----------|

## Method

1. Freeze appendix files (already this commit).
2. Run cells in order **S1-bare, S1-v3, S1-plus, S2-…** (complete a seed across conditions before the next seed).
3. Each worker pastes the condition card + the seed, nothing else from other conditions.
4. After 15 handoffs: scorer fills [scorecard.md](bakeoff-v3plus/scorecard.md).
5. Compute means, test H1–H4, write Results here.

## Key Insight

**gxp already captures almost all process lift** (Card A 14.4 → 36.0). Plus matches on simple work (collapse) and multi-file (thin path), and earns its keep on **control-plane** work: weasel/soft-close kill with a certificate (S3 B 21 → 36), Red Queen residual after a clean-looking harden (S4 B 24 → 32), gated transfer that names TP reuse and TN laser (S5 B 19 → 26). Bare fails the two seeds that invite a soft close or a leaky analogy.

Ship is the v2 pattern restated: **default to thin (v3); reach for plus on ambiguous / review / transfer-risk.** Do not wire helpers into `evaluateIdea`. Do not activate exp-015. Do not call this gxp-v4.
