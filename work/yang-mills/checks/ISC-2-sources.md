## Source B — Osterwalder–Seiler (1978), lattice RP + transfer matrix
INSPIRE abstract / Ann. Phys. 110 (1978) 440:
> "We discuss the lattice approximation of Yang-Mills fields … and we verify physical positivity for the Schwinger functions of these approximations. This implies the existence of a positive self-adjoint transfer matrix. We then prove existence and analyticity of the infinite volume limit of strongly coupled Yang-Mills theories on the lattice and we verify Wilson's confinement bound."
