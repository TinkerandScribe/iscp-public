# ISC-1 Statement — yes

**Precise restatement.** Let \(G\) be a compact Lie group (e.g. \(U(1)\) or \(SU(2)\)). In **two spacetime dimensions**, pure Yang–Mills with Wilson (or heat-kernel) action: does there exist \(\Delta>0\) such that the Hamiltonian / transfer-matrix spectrum has no points in \((0,\Delta)\) above the vacuum, and is this established by a constructive or lattice argument compatible with Osterwalder–Schrader / Osterwalder–Seiler reflection positivity?

**Numerical meaning of mass gap.** With Hamiltonian \(H\geq 0\), \(H\Omega=0\), mass gap means \(\operatorname{spec}(H)\cap(0,\Delta)=\emptyset\) for some \(\Delta>0\) (Clay / Jaffe–Witten).

**Scope.** Spacetime dimension \(=2\) only. Not \(\mathbb{R}^4\).
