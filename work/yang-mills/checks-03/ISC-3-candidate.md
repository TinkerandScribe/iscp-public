# ISC-3 Candidate — yes

**Candidate:** Osterwalder–Seiler (1978) theorem package for lattice gauge theories at strong coupling:

1. Reflection positivity of the Wilson measure (with suitable time reflections).
2. Existence of a positive self-adjoint transfer matrix.
3. Exponential decay of connected correlations of local gauge-invariant observables (mass gap / clustering) when β is small enough, uniform in volume.
4. Wilson area law (confinement) in the same window.

Applies in arbitrary spacetime dimension (including d=3,4) at **fixed** lattice spacing.
