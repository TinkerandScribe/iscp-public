# ISC-2 Sources — yes (quoted)

## Source A — Chatterjee, arXiv:1803.01950 (2018), §6 Review

> "In spite of the remarkable achievements surveyed above, there is yet no construction of a continuum limit of a lattice gauge theory in any dimension higher than two where Wilson loop variables have been shown to have nontrivial behavior."

On Balaban / Federbush UV stability:
Phase cell renormalization established ultraviolet stability for 3D/4D non-Abelian lattice theories, but Chatterjee emphasizes this has **not** yielded the continuum Wilson-loop / OS reconstruction with nontrivial continuum observables.

## Source B — Jaffe–Witten, Clay Millennium Prize Problems (2006)

Quantum Yang–Mills theory (4D): prove existence of a nontrivial continuum QFT with compact simple gauge group satisfying Wightman axioms (or equivalent) **and** a mass gap Δ>0. Status: **unsolved** (Clay Institute).

## Source C — Osterwalder–Seiler (1978) contrast

Strong-coupling **lattice** results (CARD-03) are settled; they do not supply continuum a→0 in d≥3.
